Stranglethorn Pages

A small checklist for the Green Hills of Stranglethorn quest (WoW Forever). It lists all 15 pages by chapter and shows which ones you have and where the others are.

- Green: in your bags (darker green for one copy, brighter for duplicates).
- Yellow: somewhere else — your bank, your mailbox, another of your characters, or mailed to someone.
- Gray: missing.

Duplicates get a count, so you know which spares you can sell. Pages you sell or destroy show as missing again; pages you bank or mail to an alt stay on the list. Mail sent to another character only shows while you have no other copy of that page. Right-click a page to forget its sent-mail entries.

Every character remembers what it carries, so you can check your alts' pages from any character. Bank and mailbox are updated whenever you open them. Chapters you have turned in collapse with a check mark, and the footer lists the page numbers you still need.

Open the window with the minimap button or /ghs. Hover a page for every location and when it was last seen there.
