Initial release
- Checklist of all 15 Green Hills of Stranglethorn pages, grouped by chapter.
- Shows where each page is: your bags, bank or mailbox, another of your characters, or mailed to someone.
- Count column for duplicate pages.
- Sold or destroyed pages show as missing again.
- Turned-in chapters collapse with a check mark.
- Minimap button and /ghs to open the window.
- English and German.
